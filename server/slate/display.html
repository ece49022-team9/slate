Wiring

    Display

    The picture is rebuilt from the SPI bytes the flashed firmware sends to the SSD1351. In Listen, sound on the mic makes the orb grow and turn white.

    Voice

    

    Your voice reaches Slate through the simulated firmware mic, and Slate's reply plays here. Echo feeds that reply back into the simulated mics, quieter and slightly late, the way a speaker on the device would. Use headphones so your own speakers don't add real echo.
